Why senior leaders need a different audit

What separates a recognised leader from a hidden one.

Generic personal-brand audits treat a Series A founder and a job-seeker the same way. Both get told to update their headline, add a photo, and post once a week. That advice is fine — and useless to anyone past mid-career.

At executive level the question isn't do you have a profile — it's does the right room know you? The Visibility Index is calibrated for that question. The scoring rubric weights authority signals (press mentions, board seats, speaking, podcast appearances) more heavily for executives. Tier thresholds reflect actual senior-leader visibility patterns — a Recognised Leader at 87 has earned compounding press, not just a polished LinkedIn.

The personalised moves we generate are tier-aware and tenure-aware. A 20-year veteran in the Recognised Leader tier doesn't get "write your first LinkedIn post" advice. A founder eight months into their first company doesn't get "secure a Forbes feature this quarter." Each move is calibrated to where you are and where the next-tier-up move actually lives.

FAQ

Common questions about the executive audit.

How does an executive personal brand audit differ from a general LinkedIn audit?

An executive audit is calibrated against the visibility patterns of recognised C-suite leaders, founders, and board members. The thresholds for each tier reflect what distinguishes a Hidden Gem from a Recognised Leader at executive level — not what gets a job-seeker an interview. The personalised moves take seniority, tenure, and industry into account.

Who is the executive audit designed for?

Founders, CEOs, C-suite operators, board members, NEDs, investors, senior consultants, executive directors, and senior creative leaders. Anyone whose personal brand directly affects business outcomes — fundraising, hiring, board seats, speaking, press.

Is the audit confidential?

Yes. The audit reads only public signals (your public LinkedIn, Google search results for your name, public press mentions). No private data is requested or stored. Your email is used only to deliver the PDF; we don't share it. Full privacy policy at /privacy.

Will the audit alert my network or board?

No. The audit reads your public profile from outside LinkedIn — exactly the way a journalist or analyst would. There are no logins, no API tokens, no notifications, no posts to your feed.

How is this calibrated for senior executives specifically?

The scoring rubric weights authority signals (press, board seats, speaking) more heavily for executives than for general professionals. Tier thresholds are calibrated against actual senior-leader visibility data. The personalised moves we generate are tier-aware — Recognised Leader gets sharpening moves, Hidden Gem gets foundational ones.

Who built the executive personal brand audit?

Does the audit suggest the next step?

Yes. The PDF includes three concrete moves with first-step actions, week-one milestones, 30-day success metrics, and time-to-invest. Each move is calibrated to your tier. There's also an optional 15-minute call with the Von Peach team if you want to walk through the report.
