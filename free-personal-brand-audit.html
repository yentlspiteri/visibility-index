FAQ

Common questions about the free audit.

Is the personal brand audit really free?

Yes. The score reveal is free with no signup. The PDF report is free in exchange for an email — no card, no upgrade prompt, no paywall mid-audit. We use the email to deliver the PDF and (if you opt in) a short follow-up sequence. You can unsubscribe in one click.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. The audit is a lead magnet for Von Peach FutureMakers — our personal-branding service line. If your audit surfaces three moves you'd rather not figure out alone, you can book a call. If not, keep the report and run.

What do I actually get for free?

Your composite score (0-100), tier outcome, six-dimension breakdown, three personalised moves, a 90-day plan, content prompts (writing + video) tailored to your role, and a press-target list. The full PDF runs 7 pages.

Are there hidden fees later?

No. The audit and report are fully free. Re-running the audit is also free. The only paid product in the funnel is our advisory service (FutureMakers Circle) — and that's only mentioned in the closing CTA, not pushed during the audit.

Do I need to give my credit card?

No. We never ask for payment information. Email is the only thing required to receive the PDF report.

Will my data be sold or shared?

No. We don't sell data. Your email goes to Mailchimp (for delivery) and your audit log goes to our internal Notion (for support). Both are GDPR-compliant. Full privacy policy at /privacy.

Why is it free?

Because the audit is the start of the conversation, not the end. Most people who run it don't need our advisory service — they need a clear next move. Giving the report away builds trust with the people who do.
