What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and show you relevant content. Some cookies are required for basic functionality; others we only set when you consent.

How we implement your consent

When you first visit our site, only necessary cookies are set. Meta Pixel, Contentsquare, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag are loaded only after you consent in the cookie banner — before that there are no network requests to those services. For Google Analytics we use Consent Mode v2: without consent only anonymised signals (no cookies) are sent; only after your consent do full analytics cookies get set.

Your preference is stored locally in your browser (not on our servers). You can change it at any time above by clicking "Accept all" or "Necessary only". We'll set or remove the corresponding cookies accordingly.

If you want to fully remove all cookies you can also do that directly in your browser settings — most browsers have a "Clear cookies" menu option.
