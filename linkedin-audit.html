What we check

Six things we read on your LinkedIn.

LinkedIn's own All-Star meter just checks whether fields are filled in. Our audit reads what's actually in them — whether the words mean anything, whether your photo and banner pull their weight, whether your post history shows a real rhythm, and whether the rest of the web confirms your story or contradicts it.

01 · Digital Footprint

Can people find you online?

We Google your name and check the top 10. Are those results yours, or is someone else with your name getting the airtime?

02 · Brand Clarity

Does your headline mean anything?

The 10-second test — if a stranger reads only your headline, can they tell what you do? "Transforming the future of work" doesn't pass.

03 · Authority Signals

Has anyone vouched for you?

Press mentions, podcast appearances, awards, recommendations, board seats. The proof other people speak for you.

04 · Content Cadence

Are you actually showing up?

We count your posts in the last 90 days. Cadence isn't volume — it's predictability. Once a week beats five posts in one day.

05 · Visual Identity

Does it look like you mean it?

Hi-res photo, real banner, consistent profile elements. Your face is the most-clicked thing on LinkedIn — is it working hard enough?

06 · Network Recognition

Do people actually engage?

5,000 followers and zero replies is a vanity number. We weigh real attention over loud silence.

How the audit works

What happens when you paste your LinkedIn URL.

Drop your handle. We fetch your public profile, run a Google search for your own name, scan tier-one press outlets for mentions, look at your last 90 days of posts, and measure the six dimensions. Each scores 0–3 (composite 0–18, displayed as 0–100). You see your score and tier instantly. Your PDF report — with three concrete moves and a 90-day plan — lands in your inbox in another 30 seconds.

The audit reads your public profile only. We never ask for your password or LinkedIn cookie. Same data anyone Googling you would see — we just structure it and tell you what's working.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the LinkedIn audit.

How does this LinkedIn audit differ from LinkedIn's own profile strength meter?

LinkedIn's All-Star meter only checks whether fields are filled in. Our audit reads what's actually there — your headline phrasing, banner-narrative fit, posting cadence, follower-to-engagement ratio, press signals, and whether the rest of the web confirms your story. It scores six dimensions on 0-3 (composite 0-100) and returns a tier outcome with three concrete moves.

Do I need a LinkedIn Premium account to run this audit?

No. You only need a public LinkedIn profile. We read what's already public — the same data anyone Googling you would see. We never ask for your login, password or LinkedIn cookie.

What does the LinkedIn audit actually check?

Six dimensions: Digital Footprint (whether you rank for your own name on Google), Brand Clarity (whether your headline tells a stranger what you do), Authority Signals (press, podcasts, awards), Content Cadence (your posting rhythm over 90 days), Visual Identity (photo, banner, profile polish), and Network Recognition (real engagement weighted against follower count).

How long does the LinkedIn audit take?

60 seconds end-to-end. We scrape your public LinkedIn, run a Google search for your name, check tier-one press outlets, look at your post history, and score the six dimensions. The PDF report is delivered to your inbox in another 30 seconds.

Is the LinkedIn audit really free?

Yes. The score and tier reveal cost nothing. The PDF report (with three personalised moves and a 90-day plan) is also free — we ask for an email so we can deliver it. No upgrade prompt, no paywall mid-audit, no card details required.

Can I run a LinkedIn audit on someone else's profile?

Technically yes — the tool reads any public LinkedIn URL. The PDF and personalised moves are written for the profile owner, though. If you want to audit someone else (a candidate, a board member, a competitor), you can do that, but the report will address them directly.

Will running the audit affect my LinkedIn account?

No. The audit reads your public profile from outside LinkedIn — exactly the way a Google search would. There are no logins, no API tokens, no data written back to your LinkedIn account, and no notifications to your network.
