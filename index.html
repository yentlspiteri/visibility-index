    Everything you want to know
    about your personal brand visibility

    Your Personal Brand Visibility Score is a 0–100 rating that measures how discoverable and credible you are across the channels that matter most — LinkedIn, Google search, press, and your wider digital footprint. It's calculated from 18 signals across six pillars: digital footprint, profile clarity, authority signals, content cadence, visual identity, and network reach. A higher score means you're more likely to be found, trusted, and generate inbound opportunities.
    Paste your LinkedIn profile URL, and our engine analyses your public presence in real time — your profile content, Google search footprint, press mentions, cross-platform presence, and more. Within 30–60 seconds you receive an overall score, a tier (Hidden Gem → Recognised Leader), a breakdown by pillar, and three prioritised action points. No login or credentials required.
    The audit is built specifically for founders, C-suite executives, and board members who want their personal brand to drive real business outcomes — deal flow, speaking invitations, board appointments, and talent attraction. Our scoring weights reflect an executive audience where authority and thought leadership matter more than connection volume.
    Scores fall into four tiers. Hidden Gem (0–39): significant gaps that limit inbound visibility. Rising Voice (40–59): solid foundations but inconsistent presence. Emerging Authority (60–79): visible and credible — most executives we audit land here. Recognised Leader (80–100): top-decile presence that generates consistent inbound. If you're below 60, even small fixes tend to produce measurable improvements within 30–60 days.
    Most audits complete in 30–60 seconds. We're scanning your LinkedIn profile, Google search results, press mentions, and more in real time, so speed depends on how quickly those external sources respond. You'll see a live progress indicator throughout — the wait is worth it for the depth of analysis.
    We only read publicly available information — exactly what any visitor to your LinkedIn profile or a Google search can see without logging in. We don't ask for your password, access token, or any private account data. Your score results are not sold or shared with third parties. If you choose to enter your email to receive your full report, that address is stored securely and used only for Von Peach communications.
    We recommend a fresh audit every 60–90 days, or any time you make a significant update — new role, new headline, updated photo, or a shift in posting cadence. Your score can change meaningfully within a month of implementing the recommendations, so re-running after changes is a good way to track progress.
    Von Peach is a personal branding agency specialising in executive visibility. After your audit you can book a free 15-minute walkthrough where we map the fastest path from your current score to Recognised Leader. From there, our team can handle everything from rewriting your headline and about section to building a full content strategy, brand kit, and thought-leadership programme — bespoke to your goals and industry.
