Methodology

How the Visibility Index is calculated.

The five components
Each profile is scored across five components weighted by their predictive power for executive visibility outcomes (board roles, speaking fees, inbound deal flow, candidate pull): Reach (25%), Authority (30%), Cadence (15%), Engagement (15%) and Profile Strength (15%). The composite is normalised to a 0–100 score.
Where the peer cohort comes from
The cohort is built from publicly available LinkedIn profile data of executives at comparable levels, in your industry and region. Peer benchmarks are derived from published research on executive social presence — including the Edelman Trust Barometer, BrandZ Top 100 leadership data, and the Weber Shandwick "CEO Reputation Premium" study. In v1 your ranking is calibrated against synthetic baselines from those sources. From v2 onward, it ranks against our own Visibility Index cohort (built from opted-in profiles).
Privacy & data use
Your LinkedIn URL is used to compute your score and is stored against your email address only if you choose to unlock your full report. We do not sell or share your data. You can request deletion at any time. Hosting and processing are GDPR-compliant.
What we do not measure (and why)
We deliberately exclude follower count alone, total impressions, and "viral" hit posts. These metrics reward gaming and are uncorrelated with the executive outcomes we care about. We measure cadence over volume, signal over reach, and authority over noise.
