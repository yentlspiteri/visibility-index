    Methodology

    What we actually measure.

    Six signals, all from your public LinkedIn. No surveys, no self-report. Each one scored 0–3 and summed to your 0–18 Visibility Index.

    01

    Digital Footprint

    What shows up when someone Googles your name. We check your LinkedIn presence and your top-10 search results.

    02

    Brand Clarity

    How sharp your headline and About are. An LLM scores whether a stranger could explain what you do in one line.

    03

    Authority Signals

    Press mentions, speaking, recommendations, awards. The proof that other people already vouch for you.

    04

    Content Cadence

    How often and how consistently you publish. Top-decile executives don’t post more — they post on a rhythm.

    05

    Visual Identity

    Profile photo, banner, the small craft details. Your face is the most-clicked thing on your profile.

    06

    Network Recognition

    Followers, connections, engagement on recent posts. Whether your network actually knows what you stand for.

    The technical detail (for the curious)
    Each dimension scores 0–3 against a transparent rubric and sums to a 0–18 composite that places you in one of four tiers (Hidden Gem 0–5, Rising Voice 6–10, Emerging Authority 11–15, Recognised Leader 16–18). The data sources behind each dimension:
    DimensionSource
    Digital FootprintLinkedIn profile completeness + Google search of name + company
    Brand ClarityClaude Haiku scores LinkedIn headline + About section
    Authority SignalsFeatured items, articles, recommendations, honors, press keywords in headline/About
    Content CadencePosts & articles published in the last 90 days (post-scraper actor)
    Visual IdentityProfile photo, banner image, About-section quality
    Network RecognitionFollowers, connections, recommendation count
    Peer benchmarks in v1 are calibrated against synthetic baselines from published research (Edelman Trust Barometer, BrandZ Top 100 leadership data, Weber Shandwick “CEO Reputation Premium”). From v2 onward, ranking is against the live Visibility Index cohort built from opted-in profiles.
